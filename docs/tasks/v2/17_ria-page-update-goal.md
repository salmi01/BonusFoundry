## Mandatory factual corrections

Remove or correct any generic claim that Ria gives every new customer **20 EUR/USD after a minimum transfer of 100 EUR/USD**. The official program is country-specific.

Use a directly extractable comparison table near the top of the page:

| Residence | Referrer reward | Referred friend reward | Minimum qualifying transfer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| France | EUR 20 off the referrer's next transfer | EUR 20 off the friend's first transfer | EUR 50 |
| United States | USD 20 off the referrer's next transfer | USD 10 off the friend's first transfer | USD 50 |
| Australia | AUD 30 off the referrer's next transfer | AUD 30 off the friend's first transfer | AUD 100 |

State all of the following clearly:

- The program is available to eligible residents of **France, the United States, and Australia**.
- Both the referrer and the referred friend must be at least **18 years old**.
- Ria says both people must live in one of the eligible countries. Do not infer eligibility for other countries.
- The referrer must have completed at least one transfer in the Ria app before referring friends.
- Rewards apply only to qualifying **international transfers**; domestic transfers do not qualify.
- The minimum amount must be sent in one qualifying transfer.
- The friend must be a new Ria customer with no previous Ria account or transfer history.
- The friend must enter the referral code before completing the first transfer. It cannot be applied retroactively or to a later transfer.
- The code can be entered during account creation or through “Add promo code” while preparing the first transfer.
- The friend's discount is applied to the first qualifying international transfer.
- The referrer's discount is applied to the next qualifying international transfer and may take up to two days to appear after the friend's transfer has been paid to the recipient.
- A canceled or unsuccessful transfer does not qualify. If a transfer using a referral discount is canceled, Ria says the discount becomes available for the next transfer if that transfer qualifies.
- Ria states that a referrer can refer multiple friends and receive a reward for each qualifying referral. Do not claim “unlimited” unless the current official terms explicitly use or support that wording.
- Offers, availability, amounts, and conditions can change. Tell readers to confirm the offer displayed in their Ria app before sending.
