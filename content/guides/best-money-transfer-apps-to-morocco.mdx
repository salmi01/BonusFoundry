- **Moroccan bank account:** start with the recipient's full name and bank details. Wise specifically requires a **24-digit RIB**. Compare eligible bank quotes from Wise, Taptap Send, Remitly, Ria, Paysend and Xoom; check receiving-bank charges. WorldRemit bank delivery remains unconfirmed.

## How to compare the real cost

Use two quotes taken at roughly the same time, with the **same total budget in the sender's currency** and the **same Moroccan payout method**:

1. Set a total budget, for example **€100 including all sender fees**. If an app adds a fee on top, reduce its send amount so the total debit still equals €100.
2. Record the total debit in EUR, transfer fee in EUR, quoted rate in MAD per EUR, final MAD promised, funding method and arrival estimate. Do not compare one bank-funded quote with a different card-funded quote without noting that difference.
3. Compare **MAD actually delivered**, accounting for any separately disclosed recipient-bank or cashout charge. An FX margin is already reflected in the offered rate; do not subtract it again as if it were an amount of MAD or EUR.
4. Check the result both with an eligible introductory offer and at ordinary pricing. Note whether a bonus reduces today's debit, increases today's delivery, or only provides later credit.
5. Save the quote and check again before confirming: rates, fees and available methods can change.

The €100 budget is a comparison method, not a live quote or promised MAD amount. A zero transfer fee can accompany a weaker exchange rate; the final payout is what makes equivalent quotes comparable.

## Common mistakes

- Comparing a send amount excluding fees with another provider's total debit including fees.
- Assuming a France payout method or US public campaign applies from every sending country.
- Treating a funding method, airtime top-up or later referral credit as the recipient's cash payout.
- Using an inaccurate recipient name, RIB, card number or wallet registration, or choosing a pickup location the recipient cannot reach.
- Sending more to unlock an old promotion without checking the current accepted offer.

## Troubleshooting

For a delayed transfer, check the provider tracker and quoted arrival first. Identity checks, payment authorization, bank processing, partner hours and incorrect recipient details can extend delivery. Contact the provider with the transaction reference before sending a duplicate payment.

For failed cash collection, verify the recipient's ID/name, reference or PIN, location, opening hours and collection deadline. For a missing bonus, keep the accepted offer, signup details, code/link and successful-transfer receipt; ask support to check eligibility separately from the transfer's delivery.
