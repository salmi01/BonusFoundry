Taptap Send deposits referral rewards into your account. When credit is applied to an eligible transfer, the whole reward balance increases what the recipient receives without increasing your payment. The credit expires 90 days after deposit, not 90 days after sharing a code.

Official rules checked: September 8, 2026. Sources: [referral and promo-code help](https://help.taptapsend.com/en/bonus-and-referrals/how-do-referral-codes-and-promo-codes-work), [referral programme terms](https://www.taptapsend.com/en/referrals-terms/referral-v4/referrals-en4).

## How to use an existing reward balance

1. Open the Taptap Send app and check your reward balance.
2. Choose a supported destination, recipient and delivery method.
3. Check the transaction requirements and the preview showing payment, credit used and recipient amount.
4. Confirm only when the preview matches your intended transfer.
5. Save the receipt. If the credit does not appear, check transfer type, expiry and eligibility with support.

The entire balance is applied at once; partial use is not supported. Rewards cannot be transferred, refunded or exchanged for cash. Taptap Send does not guarantee the time at which rewards will arrive in the account. See sections 2.8 and 4 of the [official referral terms](https://www.taptapsend.com/en/referrals-terms/referral-v4/referrals-en4).

## First-transfer code entry is a separate step

A new customer must enter a personal referral code in the **Have a referral code?** field while entering the first-transfer amount. Team-issued promotional codes use **ADD PROMO CODE** on the confirmation screen. Both must be entered before confirming the first transfer.

Only one code is allowed. A referral code and promo code cannot be combined, and neither can be added retroactively. The new customer must never have registered before and must complete a qualifying, uncanceled first transfer to someone other than the referrer. Confirm the minimum displayed in the app.

## When credit expires

Each reward expires **90 days after it is deposited**. Unused expired credit is removed from the balance. This rule does not establish the validity period of a referral code or campaign.

Open the Referrals area to check your current balance and route offer. Do not assume that a screenshot without an expiry date means a code is permanently valid.

## Failed transfers and wallet transfers

The [Help Center](https://help.taptapsend.com/en/bonus-and-referrals/how-do-referral-codes-and-promo-codes-work) says a failed transfer's bonus is recredited automatically. A brief delay is possible, especially if you retry immediately.

The same article excludes “wallet transfers” from bonus application and says credit remains for a later non-wallet transfer. It does not define that expression precisely enough to treat every recipient Mobile Money payout as excluded. Confirm the specific payment and delivery combination with support.

## Why credit may be missing

- **First-transfer code:** Confirm one code was attached before confirmation. A later transfer cannot repair a missed first-transfer referral.
- **New customer:** A previously registered customer does not qualify as the referred friend.
- **Minimum and route:** Check your sending country, destination, amount and campaign terms in the app.
- **Transfer status:** Qualification requires success without cancellation; a failed transfer can need time for recredit.
- **Credit expiry:** Count 90 days from deposit, not from registration or code sharing.
- **Reward delivery:** The terms do not guarantee immediate credit. Check the account and contact support when unresolved.
- **Referral limit:** The referrer earns rewards for at most 20 successful qualifying actions in a rolling 30-day period.

For account-specific help, follow [Taptap Send support instructions](https://help.taptapsend.com/en/how-do-i-contact-taptap-send-support): open your initials, Support, Contact Support, then I still need help. Include the offer screen and transfer reference.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use only part of my Taptap Send credit?

No. Taptap Send applies the entire available reward balance to an eligible transaction.

### Does the reward reduce what I pay?

The terms describe credit increasing what the recipient receives without increasing the payment taken from you. Inspect both amounts before confirming.

### Can I withdraw the reward as cash?

No. Referral rewards are account credit and are not transferable, refundable or exchangeable for cash.

### Can I combine two codes?

No. Only one referral or promo code can be attached to the first transfer. This restriction is distinct from spending an already credited reward balance.

### When does the reward expire?

The reward expires 90 days after deposit into the account. Code and campaign validity must be checked separately.

### Is the EUR offer available from my country?

The captured offer does not show a geographic scope. Taptap Send says rewards vary by sending country and destination; check the offer for your route in the app.
